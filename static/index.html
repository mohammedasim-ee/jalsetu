Treated-water exchange

Apartments and campuses with sewage treatment plants (STPs) offer surplus treated water for non-drinking uses; builders, gardens and others request it. Matching ranks offers by distance, quantity and dates, after checking the use is approved and the treatment level is acceptable. JalSetu lists and matches; it does not handle payments.

Log in to add an offer (organization accounts) or a request (any account).
